Here are some of the workshop papers and posters I have presented.
Learning Surrogates to Plan Paths for Information Gathering about Hazards in Dangerous Environments
Maryland Robotics Center Symposium, 2024

[Poster]


Trains a neural network surrogate to efficiently approximate Shannon information-theoretic planning for hazard avoidance, reducing computational cost for real-time robot missions.
Air Brake Induced Barometric Error Correction Using a Wind Tunnel Data-Driven Approach
Spaceport America Podium Talk, 2023

[Poster] [Slide deck] [Project Page]


Proposes a data-driven approach and low-pass filtering to correct barometric errors caused by airbrake deployment, improving altitude estimation in sounding rockets.
Online Feedback Motion Replanning in Dynamic Environments using Invariant Funnels
Maryland Robotics Center Symposium, 2022

[Poster] [Video]


Presents a graph structure to represent a roadmap of funnels in the higher-dimensional state space, enabling on-the-fly motion replanning. Subsequently, we leverage D*Lite for rapid rewiring of funnel paths in response to dynamic changes in the environment.
