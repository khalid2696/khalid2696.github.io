# Teaching

## Course Offerings

### Introduction to Robotics
- **Description**: An introductory course covering the fundamentals of robotics, including kinematics, dynamics, and control.
- **Prerequisites**: Basic knowledge of programming and mathematics.

### Advanced Motion Planning
- **Description**: A course focused on algorithms and techniques for motion planning in robotics.
- **Prerequisites**: Introduction to Robotics or equivalent experience.

### Control Systems
- **Description**: This course covers the principles of control theory and its applications in engineering.
- **Prerequisites**: Differential equations and linear algebra.

## Teaching Philosophy
My teaching philosophy centers around fostering a collaborative and engaging learning environment. I believe in the importance of hands-on experience and encourage students to participate actively in their learning process.

## Student Resources
- **Office Hours**: Available for one-on-one assistance and guidance.
- **Online Resources**: Access to lecture notes, assignments, and additional reading materials will be provided through the course website.

I welcome feedback from students to continuously improve the course structure and content. Please feel free to reach out with suggestions or concerns.
